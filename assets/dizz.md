### Quick tips for actually landing these
- **Personalize the bracketed parts** — generic lines get generic replies. Reference something real from her profile, story, or the conversation.
- **Match her energy** — if she's dry and witty, don't send something overly sappy. If she's playful, lean into teasing.
- **Don't spam** — one good line beats five mediocre ones sent back-to-back.
- **Follow through** — a good opener means nothing without genuine conversation after. These are icebreakers, not the whole plan.
